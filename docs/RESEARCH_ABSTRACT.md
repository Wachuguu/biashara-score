# Research Paper & Pitch Abstract: Biashara Score

Micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs)—numbering over 7.4 million businesses and contributing 33% of Kenya's GDP—are the backbone of the local economy, yet up to 85% are excluded from formal bank and SACCO working-capital credit because they operate almost entirely on mobile money (M-Pesa) and lack audited financial statements or physical collateral. Furthermore, merchants routinely mix personal and business funds on a single M-Pesa line and lose 10% to 25% of monthly revenue to uncollected customer credit recorded in fragile paper notebooks. **Biashara Score** bridges this credit and visibility gap through a mobile-first, 100% serverless AWS platform (AWS Amplify, CloudFront, API Gateway, AWS Lambda, DynamoDB, Amazon S3, EventBridge, and Amazon Bedrock) that transforms everyday M-Pesa activity into verifiable financial intelligence. By ingesting real-time till payments via Safaricom Daraja C2B webhooks as well as historical CSV statements and SMS records, the system separates personal owner draws from true operating costs using deterministic Kenyan merchant rules paired with an Anthropic Claude 3 Haiku fallback on Amazon Bedrock, while automatically reconciling incoming payments against a digital Customer Debt Ledger. These cashflows power an explainable, auditable 0–100 creditworthiness index across four pillars—Cashflow Consistency (30%), Revenue Volume (25%), Operating Margin (25%), and Debt Recovery Discipline (20%)—which generates a single-page A4 lender-ready PDF report and dispatches automated Sunday evening SMS summaries via Africa's Talking for feature-phone accessibility. To combine intuitive shopkeeper usability with institutional interoperability, Biashara Score abstracts its backend intelligence behind **Nia**, a plain-English conversational assistant for shop owners, while exposing a standardized JSON-RPC 2.0 Model Context Protocol (MCP) server (`POST /mcp`) that enables partner banks and SACCO underwriting systems to query live shop cashflow and loan readiness securely in real time.
